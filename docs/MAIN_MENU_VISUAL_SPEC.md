# Wintervale Station main menu visual specification

Reference: approved 1672 × 941 image supplied in the conversation. The image is the visual source of truth for this menu.

## Composition

- Full frame: dusk winter railway platform, viewed toward the village. Train occupies the center, village and viaduct sit behind it, station roof and clock frame the right side.
- Left sign and parchment occupy roughly x=200–740, y=30–860 in reference pixels. The sign overlaps the top of the parchment. The right side remains open to show the train and platform.
- Seven menu rows sit in the parchment from y≈320 through 788. The active Continue row is a warm gold illuminated inset; subsequent rows have brown line-art icons and delicate separators.
- Small handwritten slogan sits at lower left. A travel card overlaps the lower right of the parchment. Version text sits at lower right.

## Palette and materials

- Sunset: muted peach and dusty violet, grading into blue-purple mountain shadow.
- Snow: cool lavender and blue-white with warm amber reflections near lamps and windows.
- Wood: near-black walnut and red-brown, with visible grain, bevels, and pockets of snow.
- Parchment: cream/apricot paper with darker worn edges, subtle grain, fine border lines, and irregular lower edge.
- Text and icons: dark umber. Logo lettering: ivory. Selected button: pale gold and amber edge light.

Approximate screen colors read from the visible reference: wood `#2c1d19`, ink `#4b3025`, parchment center `#e7c5a2`, selected ivory `#ffe1a6`, warm border `#d7924a`, dusk violet `#615778`, lit window amber `#f6ad5c`.

## Scale and behavior

- Match the reference composition at 1672 × 941 as the master canvas. Preserve aspect ratio and letterbox cleanly on other aspect ratios; scale uniformly at 1920 × 1080 and 2560 × 1440.
- Keep background art, sign/parchment art, icons, labels, selection treatment, and clickable button controls in separate layers. Atmospheric snow may animate subtly.
- Use keyboard and mouse navigation. Do not include button text in the background asset.

## Architecture constraints

The current project has no menu or secondary menu screens. It starts directly in `scenes/main/main.tscn`; the quicksave system uses the default `quicksave` slot. Continue can use that system. The absent secondary screens must be reported rather than represented as complete flows.

## Implemented layers and verification

- `station_background.png`: painted clean plate generated by editing the reference to remove its interface. It is a temporary layered background, with a future live 3D menu camera possible without changing button logic.
- `wood_logo.png` and `parchment_panel.png`: generated transparent assets, kept separate from live menu labels and hit targets.
- `main_menu.gd`: seven real focusable buttons with SVG icons, uniform canvas scale, small signage, paper card, version, snowfall and navigation.
- The second pass crops the parchment's oversized carved crest so Continue stays on paper after focus moves. Seven separate ink-colored SVG icons replace the original primitive drawings, and a dedicated SVG selection frame follows keyboard or mouse focus.
- Captures from Godot 4.7.2 are stored at 1672 × 941, 1920 × 1080 and 2560 × 1440. They were inspected after two layout refinements.
- A separate Settings-focus capture checks the bug state supplied by the user: Continue must remain fully visible when another entry has focus.

## Remaining reference differences

- The generated logo uses a taller mountain crest and more elaborate wood outline; the approved lettering is larger and more flowing.
- The generated parchment has a thicker, snow-covered wooden border and more jagged lower edge than the reference.
- The train and village remain highly similar in layout and lighting, but are a generated clean plate and are not live 3D in the menu.
- The small signs, icons and travel card are reconstructed approximations. The approved icons have finer illustrated detail.
- Existing game code supplies only a quicksave slot and no new-game setup, save selector, settings, achievements or credits screen. New Game enters the current world, Continue/Load Save use quicksave, and unavailable destinations report their status within the menu.
